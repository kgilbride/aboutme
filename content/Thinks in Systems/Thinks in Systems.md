# Thinks in Systems

I find the underlying structure in messy, ambiguous situations — building frameworks, models, and analyses that give people a shared, reliable picture of reality so they can make better decisions.

This cluster shows up most strongly when the problem is diffuse, the data is fragmented, and no one has yet named what's actually going on. I do the work of making it legible.
