# About me

I'm passionate about helping middle market B2B companies build and run revenue operating systems that enable them to go-to-market more efficiently and effectively, resulting in greater customer value, increased revenue productivity, and more satisfied employees. I tend to get tapped to solve problems in three areas: (1) leadership feels stuck or misaligned; (2) work is unclear/disorganized/unprioritized; and (3) revenue engines need to be improved.

I work best with people who value operational discipline (think John McMahon and “The Qualified Sales Leader”), high quality execution (think Salesforce), data driven decision making (think Adam Grant), and strategic thinking (think Richard Rumelt).

I bring a blend of operational, technical, commercial, and financial capabilities honed from 20 years experience working with both public and private B2B companies ranging from \$40M to over \$1Bn annual revenue spanning healthcare (Aetna, athenahealth), process automation (Pegasystems, Automation Anywhere), collaboration (Mural), and education industries (Transfr).

## My mission and vision

I build bridges between current state reality to future state outcomes for people and companies by (1) clarifying the problems to be solved, (2) creating systems that drive better performance, and (3) ensuring that people are focused on work that makes the best use of their talents.

My vision is to ensure that (1) work is more purposeful and differentiated and (2) people are able to live lives of greater meaning and passion.
